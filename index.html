About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student at the Shanghai Innovation Institute (SII), jointly affiliated by Westlake University and Zhejiang University, under the supervision of Prof. Li Zhang and Prof. Peidong Liu. Before that, I obtained my Bachelor’s degree from Tongji University.

My research interests are in dexterous manipulation and visual-tactile sensing.

My research aims to develop embodied AI agents with the generalizability, dexterity, and adaptability needed to operate effectively in complex, unstructured real-world environments.
